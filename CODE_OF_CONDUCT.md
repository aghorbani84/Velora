# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct — VELORA (*Beyond Playback.*)

## 1. Our Pledge

We as members, contributors, and maintainers of **VELORA** pledge to make participation in our community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy engineering community.

## 2. Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include:
- Demonstrating empathy and kindness toward other contributors and users.
- Focusing on mathematical precision, reproducible benchmarks, and constructive code review.
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive technical feedback.
- Respecting user privacy and strictly upholding VELORA's zero-tracking, offline-first principles.

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:
- The use of sexualized language or imagery, and sexual attention or advances of any kind.
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks.
- Submitting intentionally obfuscated code, telemetry trackers, or unverified binary blobs.

## 3. Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported to the project maintainers via GitHub Security Advisories or repository discussions. All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly.
